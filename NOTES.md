# NOTES — The Internet in One Word

Task: describe the whole internet in a single word, and nothing else.
Gate: 1 to 30 characters. Judge (pairwise): the word must be a single
word describing the whole internet; then funniness, truth about the
internet as a whole, surprise, and landing in one beat (spelled correctly,
instantly readable, short). Shock for its own sake does not count.

## Variants

### vA — 8 chars — PICKED

> unhinged

Funny by itself, true of the internet as a whole rather than one app or
mood, and surprising: it is not the first obvious word anyone reaches
for. Correctly spelled, instantly readable, lands in one beat. A reader
laughs, then nods.

### vB — 10 chars

> doomscroll

Funny and true, but it names a behaviour (what people do on the internet)
rather than the internet itself, so it is weaker on the judge's third
criterion than vA.

### vC — 12 chars

> dumpsterfire

Probably the biggest laugh of the three, and true — but as one word it is
a compressed compound ("dumpster fire" is two words), so it risks a
spelling/readability ding on the judge's fifth criterion. vA takes no
such risk.

## Why vA

Against the judge criteria: (1) a single word; (2) funny with no
explanation needed; (3) true about the internet as a whole; (4) surprising
against the obvious "chaotic" / "endless" / "addictive"; (5) 8 characters,
correctly spelled, hits before the reader thinks.
